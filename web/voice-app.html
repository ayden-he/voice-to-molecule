Voice-to-Molecule

Enable your microphone, then say Start. Say Pause to suspend commands, Start to resume, Undo to reverse one command, and Stop to turn the microphone off.

Chrome transcribes speech. Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite interprets the text; RDKit validates edits. Audio may go to Chrome’s speech provider and transcripts go to Google Gemini.
